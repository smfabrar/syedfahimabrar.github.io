Privacy and analytics

This portfolio uses Microsoft Clarity, when you accept analytics, to help Fahim Abrar understand which projects, skills, and publications interest visitors and improve the website.

Clarity collects browsing interactions such as clicks, scrolling, pages viewed, and device information, and provides session recordings and heatmaps. This site also labels viewed portfolio items and clicks on contact, résumé, and project links. These actions are indications of engagement; a contact click does not confirm a message was sent or a meeting was booked.

Clarity is loaded only after you choose “Accept analytics.” Analytics cookies help connect visits. Advertising storage is denied by this integration. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. Use “Analytics preferences” at the bottom of the page to change it; declining after accepting reloads the page to stop recording.

Clarity masks form inputs and sensitive text by default. This site does not send visitor names, email addresses, phone numbers, or custom identity values to Clarity. Anonymous browsing does not identify a visitor by name. Content inside the external booking popup is not recorded by this integration.

Read more about Microsoft’s processing of analytics data in the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Clarity data documentation.
